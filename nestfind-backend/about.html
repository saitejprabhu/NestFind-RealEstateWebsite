Our Story

From a Small Startup to
India's Most Trusted
Property Platform

NestFind was founded in 2012 by Arjun Mehta and Priya Nair, two friends who had faced firsthand how broken and opaque the Indian real estate process was — full of middlemen, hidden costs, and unverified listings.

They set out to build a platform where every listing is verified, every price is transparent, and every family can make the biggest financial decision of their life with complete confidence.

Today, NestFind is India's most trusted real estate portal, operating in 200+ cities, with a team of 1,400+ people passionate about making home-ownership accessible for everyone.

Our Journey

A Decade of Milestones

2012 — Founded

NestFind Launches in Mumbai

Arjun and Priya launch the beta with just 200 listings, a 5-person team, and a big dream.

2014 — Series A

₹40 Crore Funding Round

Expanded to 20 cities after raising Series A led by Sequoia Capital India.

2017 — Milestone

1 Million Listings Crossed

Became the fastest Indian property portal to reach 1M active listings. Launched mobile app.

2020 — Pandemic Pivot

Launched Virtual Tours

Introduced 3D virtual tours, video calls with agents, and digital registration — first in India.

2023 — AI Era

Smart Match Technology

Deployed AI-powered property matching that reduced average search time from 3 months to 11 days.

2025 — Today

200+ Cities, 5M+ Families

India's most trusted real estate platform. And we're just getting started.
